Account Deletion

Camiguin Market · com.camiguinmarket.app

If you want to delete your Camiguin Market account and all of its data, there are two ways: inside the app, or by email.

1. Inside the app (easiest)

  1. Sign in to the app with your account.
  2. At the top of the screen, press the 🗑️ button.
  3. Read the warning, then press Delete.
  4. If your login is old, you will be asked to log out and log in again for security, then try again.

After that your account is permanently deleted and you are signed out.

2. By email

If you can no longer access the app, send an email requesting account deletion. Include these details:

  • The email address used for the account.
  • The subject: Account deletion request.

Send it to: YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE

We reply within 7 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Important: deletion is permanent. The account, its listings, and their photos cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (email, password, display name) Deleted from Firebase Authentication.
Listings (product name, price, description, contact, town, category) Deleted from Firestore.
Pickup location (latitude/longitude) Deleted together with the listing.

We keep no other copy of your data. Firebase backups expire after their normal retention period (up to 30 days).

If you do not want to delete your account

You can simply log out and stop using the app. Your data stays until you ask for deletion.
